About Aurova Insure

A dependable right hand for busy UK businesses

We started Aurova Insure because we kept seeing the same thing: capable people in small businesses losing hours every week to admin that has to be done, but doesn't need to be done by them.

What we believe

Good admin support isn't about cheap labour. It's about reliability: work that gets done properly, on time, the same way every time, by people who understand why it matters to your business.

So we work as an extension of your team, not as a faceless call centre or a marketplace of freelancers. We agree the work in writing, follow your processes, keep you informed and take responsibility for what we've agreed to do.

Why the name?

"Insure" is about making sure the work is done right before it comes back to you. We are not an insurer and we don't sell or arrange insurance of any kind.

Our focus

We started with UK letting agents, where the Renters' Rights Act has added to an already heavy admin load, and we also support tradespeople, accountants, professional firms and other small businesses.

Data protection and security

How we protect your information

We'd rather explain our approach clearly than show badges. Here is what we do and where each measure stands today.

Who is responsible for what

When we work for you, you are usually the controller of your customers' personal data, and we act as your processor. That means we only use the data to do the work you've asked for, following your instructions, under a written data processing agreement as UK GDPR requires.

Access from India

Our delivery team is in India, so personal data in your systems is accessed from outside the UK. India doesn't currently have a UK "adequacy" decision, so we use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, with a transfer risk assessment, to provide the safeguards UK GDPR requires. We never claim that your data stays in the UK.

If something goes wrong

We tell you promptly about any actual or suspected personal data breach, with the details you need, so you can meet your own duty to report to the ICO within 72 hours where required.
