Part B: How we provide our services (summary)

7. Nature of our services

We provide administrative support to businesses. We carry out clearly defined tasks, agreed in writing, following our clients' processes and instructions. We do not provide legal, financial, accounting, tax, insurance or other regulated services or advice, and we do not give professional sign-off.

8. Scope

The tasks, standards, volumes and working hours for each client are set out in a written service description. Work outside that scope is agreed separately before we start.

9. Responsibilities

  • We are responsible for carrying out the agreed tasks with reasonable skill and care, supervising our team and keeping the client informed.
  • The client is responsible for decisions, approvals and its legal and regulatory obligations to its own customers, for giving us accurate instructions and the access we need, and for anything it chooses to do with our work.

10. Confidentiality and data protection

We keep our clients' information confidential and use it only to provide the services. Where we process personal data for a client, we do so as their processor, under a data processing agreement that meets UK GDPR, including the safeguards for transfers to our team in India. See our privacy policy.

11. Fees and payment

Fees are agreed in writing before work starts and are usually invoiced monthly. Payment terms are set out in each client's agreement.

12. Term and ending the arrangement

The minimum term and notice period are set out in each client's agreement. When an arrangement ends, we remove our access to the client's systems and return or delete their data.

13. Limits on liability

Each client agreement sets out how liability is shared and capped. Nothing limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
