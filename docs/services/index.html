Our services

Administrative support, clearly defined

We take on routine work that follows clear rules, and we do it inside your systems. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Service categories

01

Property administration

  • Compliance diary: certificate and licence dates tracked and chased
  • Tenancy documents prepared from your templates for approval
  • Maintenance requests logged and contractors booked
  • Property, landlord and tenant records kept up to date
More on letting agency support
02

Inbox and correspondence management

  • Shared inboxes sorted, labelled and routed every day
  • Routine replies sent from your approved wording
  • Anything needing judgement flagged to the right person
  • Follow-ups tracked so conversations don't go quiet
03

Customer and tenant administration

  • New customer or tenant onboarding: forms, documents and welcome emails
  • Routine queries answered and logged
  • Updates and reminders sent at the right time
04

CRM and database management

  • New records added and existing ones updated
  • Duplicates merged and missing details filled in
  • Notes and activity logged after calls and emails
  • Simple data checks and clean-ups
05

Document and record management

  • Letters, forms and documents prepared from your templates
  • Files named, organised and stored where they belong
  • Missing documents identified and chased
06

Invoicing and payment administration

  • Invoices and statements raised in your accounting software
  • Polite, persistent payment reminders
  • Payments matched and outstanding balances reported

We don't move money or approve payments. That stays with you.

07

Scheduling and coordination

  • Diary management and appointment booking
  • Contractors and suppliers booked and confirmed
  • Reminders sent to customers before appointments
08

General business administration

  • Data entry and spreadsheet upkeep
  • Weekly reports and simple dashboards
  • Research and list building
  • The one-off jobs that keep getting pushed back
