What we check

Every property, checked on the same 10 points

These are the records that matter most since the Renters' Rights Act came into force on 1 May 2026, and the ones most often missing from a busy agency's files.

Tenancy and deposit

  • Tenancy agreement or written statement of terms on file
  • Renters' Rights Act Information Sheet given to existing tenants, with a record of when
  • Deposit protected, within the 5-week cap, with prescribed information served
  • Rent increases made by the statutory notice, no more than once a year
  • A dated record that a Right to Rent check was done (we never ask for ID documents)

Safety and licensing

  • Gas safety record in date (every 12 months)
  • Electrical safety report (EICR) in date (every 5 years)
  • EPC on file and rated E or better
  • Smoke and carbon monoxide alarm checks recorded
  • HMO or selective licence in place where the property needs one
How it works

From first call to report in about a week

  1. Quick call

    A 15-minute call to agree which 25 properties to check and which files you'll send.

  2. Paperwork first

    We sign a confidentiality and data processing agreement with you before any files are shared.

  3. You export, we check

    You send exported files from your CRM. Our team works through them, and a person checks every finding.

  4. Your report

    Within 5 working days of receiving the files, you get the report and a 20-minute call to talk it through.

What you get

A report you can act on the same day

  • A one-page summary: how many properties are green, amber and red
  • The issues to fix first, in order of urgency, with the evidence needed to close each one
  • A property-by-property register you can keep using
  • Upcoming expiry dates for the next 90 days

Questions

About the free health check

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no charge and no obligation to buy anything afterwards. We offer it because it's the clearest way to show you how we work.

Why only 25 properties?

Every finding is checked by a person, so each health check takes real time. Twenty-five properties is enough to show the pattern across a portfolio. If you'd like the rest checked, we'll quote for a full audit.

Which files will you need?

Usually an export of tenancies and properties from your CRM, your certificate records (gas, EICR, EPC), deposit scheme records and copies of the documents given to tenants. We'll send you a short checklist after the first call.

Will you contact my landlords or tenants?

No. We work only with you. Nothing is sent to anyone else and nothing is changed in your systems.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's an administrative review of whether the expected records are on file and in date. Where something needs a legal view, we'll say so and you can take advice from your own solicitor or professional body.
