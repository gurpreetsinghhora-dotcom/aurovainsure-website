From first conversation to work done, step by step

Outsourcing works when everyone knows exactly what's being done, by whom and to what standard. That's why we take the setup seriously.

The process

  1. Initial discussion

    A short call about your business, your team and the admin that's taking up your time. No obligation, and we'll be honest if we're not the right fit.

  2. Identify the workload

    We look at the tasks in detail: how often they happen, how long they take, which systems they use and where things currently get stuck.

  3. Agree responsibilities and processes

    We write down each task, the standard expected, what we decide and what we always refer to you. You approve this before we start.

  4. Set up secure access and ways of working

    You give our team named logins to the systems we need, with only the access each task requires. We sign a data processing agreement and agree how we'll communicate.

  5. Allocate the right people

    We assign team members to your account and train them in your processes, your templates and your tone of voice.

  6. Begin work

    Work starts on the agreed tasks, with closer checking in the first weeks while everyone settles in.

  7. Review and improve

    We report regularly on what's been done and what's outstanding, review the work with you and refine the processes as we learn your business.

Your systems, your rules

We fit into the way you already work

We don't ask you to change software or move your data. Our team works as named users inside your existing tools, such as your CRM, email, shared drives, accounting and maintenance systems, following your processes and templates.

That keeps your data where it belongs, makes our work visible to you and means you can remove our access at any time.

What you can expect from us

  • A named UK contact for your account
  • Written task instructions you've approved
  • Agreed rules for what we escalate, and how fast
  • A regular summary of work done and outstanding
  • Holiday and absence cover within our team
