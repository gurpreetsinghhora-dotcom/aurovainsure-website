1. Who we are

Aurova Insure is a trading name of {{company}}, a company registered in England and Wales with company number {{company_no}}. Our registered office is {{reg_office}}.

For the personal information described in sections 2 and 3, we are the controller. Our registration with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) is in progress, and we will publish our registration number here once it is issued.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiries: when you contact us through the website, by email or by phone, we collect your name, business name, email address, telephone number (if you give it), your type of business and the details of your enquiry.
  • Clients and their staff: names, job titles and business contact details of the people we deal with at our clients, plus our correspondence and records of the work we do.
  • Website visits: our website host records technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, in server logs for security and to keep the site running. We use cookie-free analytics to count visits (see section 10).

Please don't send us sensitive personal information, or details about your own customers, through the enquiry form.

4. Data we handle for our clients

When we provide administrative support, we often access personal data held in our clients' systems, such as their own customers', tenants' or landlords' details. In that work, our client is the controller and we act as their processor. We use that data only to carry out the work our client has instructed, under a written data processing agreement, and the client's own privacy notice explains how it is used. If your information is held by one of our clients, please contact them first about your rights.

5. Who we share it with

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • Our delivery team in India: client work is carried out by our operations team in Mumbai, run by SBR Ventures (Mumbai), a business under the same ownership. They access personal data only to carry out client work, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Service providers: our website host (GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub Inc.), our email provider (Apple iCloud), our website analytics provider (Cloudflare Web Analytics, operated by Cloudflare, Inc.) a form-delivery service that passes website enquiries to our inbox (Web3Forms) and an online booking service for discovery calls (Cal.com, operated by Cal.com, Inc.).
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, and authorities where the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Some of the people and providers above are outside the UK:

  • India: our delivery team. India doesn't currently have a UK adequacy decision, so we protect transfers with the UK International Data Transfer Agreement issued by the ICO, supported by a transfer risk assessment.
  • United States and elsewhere: some of our service providers process data in the US. We rely on the UK–US data bridge where the provider is certified, or on the safeguards in the provider's own data transfer terms.

You can ask us for more information about these safeguards using the details in section 11.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client contracts, correspondence and financial records: for the length of the contract and 6 years afterwards, to meet legal and tax requirements.
  • Data we process for clients: only for as long as the client instructs. When work ends, our access is removed and any working copies are deleted or returned.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. These include named user accounts, only the access each task needs, multi-factor authentication where available, confidentiality agreements and data protection training for our team. If a personal data breach occurs, we act quickly to contain it and notify the people and authorities we need to. Where we are a processor, we tell the client promptly so they can meet their own obligations.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to our use of it, including for marketing
  • ask us to transfer information you gave us to another organisation, where this applies

We'll respond within one month. There's normally no charge.

10. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or tracking tools. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, provided by Cloudflare, Inc., to count visits and see which pages are read and where visitors came from. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device, and does not track you across websites or build a profile of you. Cloudflare processes basic technical information, such as your browser type and the page visited, to produce these totals, and we only see combined figures, not individual visitors. Our fonts are hosted on our own website, so viewing a page doesn't send your details to font providers.
