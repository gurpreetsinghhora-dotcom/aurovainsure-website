Data protection and confidentiality

Your information, handled with care

Clients trust us with access to their systems and their customers' details. We treat that as the most important part of the job, and we're transparent about how it works.

Because our team is in India, client data is accessed from outside the UK. We put that in writing, with the safeguards UK GDPR requires.

How we protect data
  • A written data processing agreement with every client
  • We work inside your systems, as named users with only the access we need
  • Personal data transfers to India covered by the UK's International Data Transfer Agreement
  • Confidentiality agreements and data protection training for every team member
  • Breaches reported to you promptly, so you can meet your own legal duties
  • Access removed and working files deleted when work ends
Questions

Frequently asked questions

What kind of work can you take on?

Routine, repeatable administration with clear rules: inbox management, data entry, CRM updates, diary and appointment management, document preparation, invoicing and payment reminders, and property management admin for letting agents. We agree the exact tasks with you in writing before we start.

Where is the work done?

Your contract and point of contact are in the UK. The day-to-day work is done by our supervised team in Mumbai, India, on hours aligned to the UK working day.

Do you use your own software?

No. We work inside the systems you already use, such as your CRM, email and maintenance platforms, as named users with their own logins. Your data stays in your systems and you can remove our access at any time.

Is my data safe if it is accessed from India?

UK GDPR allows personal data to be accessed from outside the UK when the right safeguards are in place. We sign a data processing agreement with every client and use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, alongside access controls, confidentiality agreements and staff training.

Do you give legal or accounting advice?

No. We provide administrative support only. Decisions about legal notices, referencing, compliance sign-off, accounts and tax stay with you and your professional advisers.

Are you an insurance company?

No. Aurova Insure is an administration business. We use "Insure" in the sense of making sure work is done right. We don't sell or arrange insurance.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the volume and type of work. We offer monthly support, flexible ad-hoc help and dedicated support packages. After a short call we'll give you a clear written quote.
