Data protection and security

How we protect your information

We'd rather explain our approach clearly than show badges. Here is what we do and where each measure stands today.

Who is responsible for what

When we work for you, you are usually the controller of your customers' personal data, and we act as your processor. That means we only use the data to do the work you've asked for, following your instructions, under a written data processing agreement as UK GDPR requires.

Access from India

Our delivery team is in India, so personal data in your systems is accessed from outside the UK. India doesn't currently have a UK "adequacy" decision, so we use the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, with a transfer risk assessment, to provide the safeguards UK GDPR requires. We never claim that your data stays in the UK.

If something goes wrong

We tell you promptly about any actual or suspected personal data breach, with the details you need, so you can meet your own duty to report to the ICO within 72 hours where required.
